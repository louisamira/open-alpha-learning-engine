# Product

Open Alpha Learning Engine is an open-source, self-improving adaptive learning engine for mastery learning.

The durable product asset is a validated curriculum graph plus learner model, assessment engine, lesson renderer, review pipeline, and reference learning UI. The graph should be useful even when the learning surface changes.

## Product Thesis

Learners should get a focused mastery cockpit:

- A diagnostic starting point
- A ranked next-best concept queue
- A lesson grounded in reviewed artifacts
- A tutor constrained to the concept source of truth
- Practice that adapts after each attempt
- Mastery checks with evidence
- Review prompts for retention
- Timeback and focus feedback that rewards efficient, honest study

Contributors and agents should get:

- Public graph artifacts
- Clear schemas
- A ranked gap queue
- Deterministic validation
- Review decisions with accept, reject, or improve outcomes
- Reputation from accepted contributions and downstream learner outcomes

The contribution model should feel closer to Wikipedia than to a closed course CMS: curriculum nodes are reviewed artifacts, improvements are small and reversible, source notes matter, and validation runs before changes reach learners.

## Orientation Essay Layer

Each track, unit, and major concept can include a short optional orientation essay. These essays are 3 to 4 minute reads that explain:

- What the subject or idea is
- Where it came from historically
- What human or social problem it solves
- Why it matters
- What changes when you understand it
- What learners can safely ignore for now
- How to go deeper

These essays are curriculum graph artifacts and should be reviewed like lesson content.
